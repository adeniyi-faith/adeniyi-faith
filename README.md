## 👋 About me

I'm a full-stack developer with **7 years** of experience turning business ideas into working software. I run **GetOnline Studio**, a web development and technology agency, where I design, build, deploy and look after the whole product myself, from the database to the screen the customer sees.

My strongest areas:

- 💳 **Fintech and lending systems:** payments, repayments, approvals, and keeping money-related data correct
- 🔐 **Security and reliability:** safe logins, protected data, backups, and servers that stay up
- ⚡ **Performance:** replacing slow, bloated websites with fast custom-built ones
- 🤖 **AI and automation:** tools that save people hours of repeated work
