## 👋 About me

Hi, I'm Faith. I've been building for the web for **7 years**, turning business ideas into software people actually use. As Lead Developer at **GetOnline Studio**, I look after the whole product, from the database to the screen the client sees.

These days, most of my work is **AI-assisted**. AI does a lot of the heavy lifting: writing code, drafting features and speeding up the routine work. That frees me to spend my time on what matters most: deciding what to build, checking that it's right and safe, and making sure it actually works for the people using it. Faster delivery, and more time for the parts that need a human.

What I enjoy most:

- **Fintech and lending systems:** payments, repayments, approvals, and keeping money-related data correct
- **Security and reliability:** safe logins, protected data, backups, and servers that stay up
- **Performance:** replacing slow, bloated websites with fast custom-built ones
- **AI and automation:** workflows that save people hours of repeated work

---

## 🤝 What I build for clients

- **Business platforms:** lending, payments, wallets and admin dashboards
- **Logistics and operations apps:** orders, riders, stock and daily reports
- **School and organisation systems:** records, payments and communication
- **Websites:** fast, custom WordPress themes and plugins, plus PHP and React sites
- **Mobile apps:** built with React Native
- **AI features:** chat assistants and automation that fit into the client's workflow
